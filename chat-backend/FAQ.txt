What is Booster Bookstore?
Booster Bookstore is an online platform where readers can easily browse, discover, and purchase books across all genres. Our goal is to make book shopping simple, enjoyable, and accessible to everyone.

Do you sell physical books or eBooks?
We offer both! Customers can purchase physical copies shipped directly to their address or digital eBooks that can be downloaded instantly after checkout.

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept major credit and debit cards, PayPal, and Booster Gift Cards. All transactions are securely processed to protect your information.

How long does shipping take?
Shipping typically takes 3-5 business days for domestic orders and 7-14 days for international orders. You'll receive a tracking number once your order has shipped.

Do you offer free shipping?
Yes! Orders over $50 qualify for free standard shipping within the United States.

Can I return or exchange a book?
Absolutely. If your book arrives damaged or incorrect, you can return it within 14 days of delivery for a full refund or replacement. Digital downloads, however, are non-refundable once accessed.

How can I track my order?
After checkout, you'll receive an email confirmation with a tracking link. You can also check your order status by logging into your Booster Bookstore account and visiting My Orders.

How can I contact customer support?
You can reach our support team by emailing support@boosterbookstore.com or visiting the Contact Us page on our website. We typically respond within 24 hours.
